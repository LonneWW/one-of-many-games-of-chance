# One of Many Games of Chance

This is a dumb simple game I chose to make in little less than two weeks. For the time being it's just a visual test for the complete game I'll make in a long, distant future. I promise it will be nicer to look at once finished.

A simple multiplayer browser game: two players choose a suit during the same round and then reveal their choices. The result depends on the chosen suit.

To play, open [this page](https://lonneww.github.io/one-of-many-games-of-chance/) in a browser. One player shares their Peer ID, and the other enters it to connect.

The game uses PeerJS to connect players. **Opera is not currently supported**; use Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
